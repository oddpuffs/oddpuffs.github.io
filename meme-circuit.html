MEME CIRCUIT

"Chaos creates evolution."

The Meme Circuit is the most unpredictable faction in Arcadia-7.

They weaponize humor, viral ideas, and social disruption.

What begins as a joke often becomes reality around them. Many possess unstable emotional memory shards.

Beliefs

• Order creates stagnation. • Chaos reveals possibilities.

• Innovation comes from unpredictability.

Territory

Rapidly changing meme zones and viral hubs.

Reputation

Brilliant, ridiculous, and impossible to control.

Meme Circuit transforms unpredictability into power, turning the absurd into opportunities that reshape Arcadia-7.

Their creativity spreads faster than any virus, inspiring unexpected alliances, innovations, and moments of pure chaos.

No one can predict their next move, making them as entertaining as they are dangerous. Where others see disorder, the Meme Circuit sees the beginning of the next evolution.
