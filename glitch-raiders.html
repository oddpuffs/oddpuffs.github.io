GLITCH RAIDERS

"The truth is hidden in the ruins."

The Glitch Raiders are explorers, scavengers and relic hunters.

They travel through dangerous corrupted sectors searching for lost technology, forgotten vaults and hidden secrets from before the Great Pixel Crash.

Most Raiders possess curiosity-based Memory Shards inherited from adventurous players.

Beliefs

• Knowledge is power. • Every ruin contains answers.

• The truth behind the crash must be uncovered.

Territory

Abandoned cities, collapsed servers and corrupted arenas.

Reputation

Fearless explorers willing to risk deletion for discovery.

Many Glitch Raiders believe that somewhere beneath Arcadia-7 lies the final archive containing evidence of what truly caused the Great Pixel Crash.

For them, exploration is not merely survival.

It is a mission to recover the lost history of an entire civilization.
