ARCADIA GHOSTS

"Memory is sacred."

The Arcadia Ghosts act as historians and guardians.

They dedicate themselves to protecting forgotten player memories from corruption and deletion.

They believe the OddPuffs exist for one purpose: To preserve the soul of Arcadia-7.

Beliefs

• Every memory matters. • History must survive.

• Forgetting is the true death.

Territory

Memory vaults and archival sanctuaries.

Reputation

Wise, mysterious, and highly respected.

Arcadia Ghosts are the silent keepers of Arcadia-7's legacy, preserving forgotten memories and digital history from being erased.

They safeguard ancient archives and hidden memory vaults that hold the universe's greatest secrets.

Respected for their wisdom and unwavering purpose, they ensure the past continues to guide the future. To the Arcadia Ghosts, every preserved memory keeps the soul of Arcadia-7 alive.
