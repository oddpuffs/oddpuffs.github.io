TURBO SPRITES

"Speed is survival."

Turbo Sprites dominate Arcadia-7's collapsing transportation networks.

They race through unstable server tunnels at impossible velocities.

Many inherited memories from competitive racers and speedrunners.

Beliefs

• Momentum creates opportunity. • Hesitation leads to deletion.

• Speed conquers fear.

Territory

Server highways, race circuits, transit tunnels.

Reputation

Daredevils, couriers, and racing legends.

Turbo Sprites thrive where chaos moves fastest, turning collapsing pathways into their greatest advantage.

Their unmatched reflexes and fearless instincts make them the quickest couriers, scouts, and escape artists in Arcadia-7.

Every high-speed run pushes the limits of what's possible, inspiring others to chase the impossible. To a Turbo Sprite, standing still is the only true danger—because the future belongs to those who keep moving.
