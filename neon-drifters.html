NEON DRIFTERS

"The journey matters more than the destination."

The Neon Drifters wander endlessly across Arcadia-7.

Rather than settling in one place, they travel between regions following mysterious signals, glitches, and rumors.

Many believe they can hear echoes of the universe itself.

Beliefs

• Freedom above all. • No server should control destiny.

• The universe is meant to be explored.

Territory

Everywhere and nowhere.

Reputation

Nomads, storytellers, and messengers.

Neon Drifters are freedom-loving explorers who roam Arcadia-7 chasing mysteries, signals, and hidden discoveries.

Known for their adaptability and curiosity, they uncover secrets where others see chaos.

For a Neon Drifter, every journey is an adventure and every glitch may reveal a new path forward.
