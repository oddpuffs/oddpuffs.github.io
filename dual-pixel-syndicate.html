DUAL PIXEL SYNDICATE

"Everything has a price."

The Dual Pixel Syndicate controls the underground economy of Arcadia-7.

They operate hidden marketplaces where rare skins, memory shards, artifacts, and forbidden code are traded.

Many of their leaders possess memories from traders, collectors, and market manipulators.

Beliefs

• Information is currency. • Wealth creates influence.

• Control the market, control the future.

Territory

Black-market hubs and encrypted trade networks.

Reputation

Powerful, wealthy, and extremely dangerous.

Dual Pixel Syndicate thrives in the shadows, where influence is earned through strategy, secrets, and calculated deals

Masters of negotiation and manipulation, they shape the balance of power without ever stepping onto the battlefield.

Their vast network of encrypted traders and informants keeps them several moves ahead of every rival faction. In Arcadia-7, if something rare exists, the Syndicate already knows its value—and who wants it.
