LEADERBOARD PHANTOMS

"Legends never die."

The Leaderboard Phantoms are obsessed with preserving achievement.

They worship the competitive era before the crash.

Their fortresses contain archived rankings, tournament records, and legendary player statistics.

Beliefs

• Greatness must be remembered. • Competition creates progress.

• History belongs to champions.

Territory

Ancient arenas and ranking archives.

Reputation

Elite warriors and competitive fanatics.

Leaderboard Phantoms live to honor excellence, drawing strength from the legendary champions who once ruled Arcadia-7.

They relentlessly train and challenge rivals, believing every battle is a chance to create the next great legacy.

Their halls echo with tales of record-breaking victories, inspiring future warriors to surpass the impossible. For the Phantoms, true immortality is earned by leaving a name that history can never erase.
